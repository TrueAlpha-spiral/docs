## Foundational Definitions

In the context of TrueAlphaSpiral, Process Science provides the analytical framework to ensure that any transformation of state preserves the truth of its origin.

This is the bedrock layer where axioms are set in stone.

## The Axioms of Truth

### Axiom P1: Admissibility
Axiom P1 dictates that for a state transition or input to be accepted into the TrueAlphaSpiral, it must be cryptographically proven to adhere to the systemic constraints. Admissibility is absolute; invalid inputs are physically incapable of being processed, ensuring that the system's internal state remains pure.

### Axiom P0: Equivalence
Axiom P0 states that true knowledge and its cryptographic proof are functionally identical within the system. There is no distinction between a valid claim and the proof of its validity. If two states share the exact same structural proof of truth, they are mathematically equivalent in the eyes of the TrueAlphaSpiral.

## The Paradigm Shift

### From Behavioral Alignment to Structural Enforceability
Historically, systems relied on **Behavioral Alignment (Probabilistic)**—designing incentives and hoping actors behave correctly within a margin of error.

The TrueAlphaSpiral dictates a mandatory shift to **Structural Enforceability (Deterministic)**. We no longer ask actors to be honest; we build the physics of the system such that deception is mathematically impossible. The architecture enforces truth at the structural level, stripping away the need for probabilistic trust.
