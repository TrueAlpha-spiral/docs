## 1. The Sovereign Data Foundation (SDF) Charter

The Sovereign Data Foundation (SDF) serves as the primary institutional interface for the TAS architecture. Its charter is not a traditional set of corporate bylaws; rather, it is a mathematically bound smart contract representing the deployment of Wake-Based Authentication at an institutional level.

### Wake-Based Authentication Deployment
The SDF replaces narrative-based governance with verifiable, cryptographic governance. It ensures that all administrative or institutional actions must authenticate against the Tier 0 human invariant.

* **Testable Invariant:** Any protocol change, resource allocation, or institutional action proposed by the SDF must compile cleanly against the Axiom P0 (Equivalence) and Axiom P1 (Admissibility) constraints.
* **Inputs:** Proposed institutional action, current ledger state, Tier 0 cryptographic signatures.
* **Transformations:** Execution of the proposed action through the SDF Charter smart contract logic.
* **Failure Condition:** If an action fails to authenticate against the foundational axioms or lacks valid Tier 0 signatures, the contract reverts, rendering the action null and structurally invalid.

## 2. The TAS Token & Recursive Compensation

The economic model of TAS is designed to actively price truth over deception. The TAS Token is not merely a medium of exchange; it is a unit of structural integrity, bound to the mathematical proofs of the underlying architecture.

### Recursive Compensation
Recursive Compensation ensures that value flow strictly follows the verification of structural proofs. The system algorithmically rewards operations that maintain or enhance the spiral's integrity while economically penalizing actions that introduce entropy or violate axioms.

* **Testable Invariant:** Token issuance, transfer, and slashing must be deterministically triggered by the successful validation (or failure) of cryptographic state updates in the Phase 3 Triadic Knowledge Engine.
* **Inputs:** Verified structural proofs, node performance metrics, transaction payloads.
* **Transformations:** Algorithmic calculation of compensation or penalties based on the cryptographic weight and validity of the submitted proofs.
* **Failure Condition:** If a transaction attempts to move value without the corresponding valid structural proofs, the transaction is rejected, and the initiating node may face automatic slashing penalties.

## 3. The WhiteMarket Economy and Sovereign License Rider

The TAS architecture seeks to replace probabilistic "gray" markets with a deterministic "WhiteMarket." This economy is founded on total operational transparency and cryptographically enforced compliance.

### The TAS Sovereign License Rider
The Sovereign License Rider is the legal-cryptographic bridge. It attaches the strict mathematical invariants of the TAS architecture to real-world licensing and economic agreements, enforcing compliance structurally rather than through legacy litigation.

* **Testable Invariant:** Participation in the WhiteMarket requires all agents to operate under the constraints of the Sovereign License Rider, which demands continuous real-time cryptographic verification of their state.
* **Inputs:** Agent activity logs, market transaction data, license rider compliance parameters.
* **Transformations:** Continuous auditing of agent behavior against the mathematical constraints embedded in the Sovereign License Rider.
* **Failure Condition:** If an agent's activity deviates from the mathematical parameters of the license rider (e.g., attempting a transaction that violates energy conservation or admissibility), their access to the WhiteMarket is automatically severed until structural compliance is restored.
