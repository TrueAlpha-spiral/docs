## 1. The Nordland Hamiltonian ($\mathcal H_N$)

The Nordland Hamiltonian is the central mathematical representation of total system energy and constraint within TAS. It models the system as a closed energetic environment where operational validity is intrinsically tied to energy conservation.

### Energy Conservation Constraint
The fundamental constraint enforced by the Nordland Hamiltonian is that the change in total energy with respect to time must be zero: $\frac{d\mathcal H_N}{dt}=0$. Any operation that introduces "unbacked" energy (or removes it) violates this conservation principle and is computationally impossible to express.

* **Testable Invariant:** For any state transition, the energy of the system before the transition must equal the energy of the system after the transition.
* **Inputs:** Current state vectors, proposed state update values.
* **Transformations:** Evaluation of the proposed update against the Nordland Hamiltonian equation.
* **Failure Condition:** If the transition results in $\frac{d\mathcal H_N}{dt} \neq 0$, the operation is immediately rejected as an illegal structural modification.
