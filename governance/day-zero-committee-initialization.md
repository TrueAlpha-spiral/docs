# Day Zero Committee Initialization

## Status

Committee slate is currently blank.

That blank state is not a failure condition. It is the first evidentiary condition: no authority is presumed before attestation.

## Operational Charge

The Day Zero committee exists to validate and steward the launch boundary defined by the SDF standard:

> No Attestation → No Execution

The committee must not function as a symbolic advisory board. It must function as an execution-legitimacy review surface.

## Initial Charges

1. Verify that the Day Zero Readiness Memo is publicly inspectable.
2. Define the minimum witness requirements for SDF authority.
3. Establish reviewer roles for cryptographic provenance, legal scope, economic settlement, and refusal integrity.
4. Confirm that Zero-Token Refusal is testable as a pre-generation gate.
5. Require that every committee seat be filled by explicit attestation, not assumption.
6. Define public receipt requirements for committee actions.
7. Maintain a blank-seat doctrine: an unfilled seat has no delegated authority.

## Role Categories

- Cryptographic Provenance Reviewer
- Refusal Integrity Reviewer
- WhiteMarket Settlement Reviewer
- Legal Scope Reviewer
- Public Receipt Reviewer
- Human Provenance Witness
- Stewardship Chair

## Blank Committee Doctrine

A blank committee is preferable to a falsely populated committee.

A committee member exists only after a verifiable appointment record, scope definition, and acceptance attestation exist.

No implied authority.
No invisible board.
No inherited legitimacy without receipt.

## Launch Constraint

The Day Zero committee may not certify readiness until its own authority structure satisfies the same rule it imposes on execution:

> No Attestation → No Execution

## Closing Principle

The first committee action is refusal of presumed authority.
